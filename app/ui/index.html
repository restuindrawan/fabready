    Get your PCB ready to order

    fabready checks routing, fab limits, parts and the board outline, plus extra checks for keyboards, and re-checks every time you save in KiCad. Where are you starting from?

    Try a sample board

    Each sample opens as a fresh copy, so you can edit, fix and break it freely. Its footprints are simplified stand-ins, so don't order it.

    Connect AI agents

    The Agent tab chats with Claude Code, Codex or OpenCode right here. If you'd rather use your own terminal or editor, this app is also an MCP server: connected agents can check the board, see it, apply fabready's automatic fixes (list_fixes, apply_fix) and fix the rest by editing your KiCad files, while the app re-checks after every save.
